    ## Living in Recoleta

    Recoleta is a residential and commercial neighbourhood of Asunción, next to Villa Morra. It is framed by República Argentina, Mariscal López and Choferes del Chaco avenues, and by Venezuela and Chaco Boreal streets. It borders Villa Morra to the north, Tembetary to the south, Mariscal Estigarribia to the east and Mburicaó to the west.

    The neighbourhood takes its name from the Recoleta Cemetery, which covers a large part of its area, and from the church of La Recoleta, built under the government of Carlos Antonio López. It is also home to several embassies, the Infante Rivarola and Manuel Ortiz Guerrero squares and many schools.

    The avenues are paved with asphalt and many inner streets are cobbled, which gives the area a quiet pace just a few blocks from Villa Morra and Shopping Mariscal.
