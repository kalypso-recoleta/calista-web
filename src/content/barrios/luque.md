    ## Living in Luque

    Luque is a city in the Central department that borders Asunción. According to the 2022 census, it is the third most populous city in the country and the most populous in the Central department.

    It is home to Silvio Pettirossi International Airport, Paraguay's main airport. Nearby are the CONMEBOL headquarters, with its convention centre and the South American Football Museum, Ñu Guazú Park and the Paraguayan Olympic Committee.

    Known as the "city of music", Luque is famous for its jewellery and for making guitars and harps. For frequent travellers, living close to the airport is a practical advantage.

    ## Why live in Luque

    - **Close to the airport:** Silvio Pettirossi is in the city.
    - **Close to Asunción:** near Madame Lynch Avenue and the capital's "new centre".
