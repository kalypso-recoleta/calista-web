    ## Living in Lambaré

    Lambaré is a city in the Central department and part of Greater Asunción. It borders Asunción to the west and north, Villa Elisa to the south-east and, to the south-west, the Paraguay River, which separates it from Argentina.

    One of its main avenues is Cacique Lambaré. Close by, on the riverbank, stands Cerro Lambaré, topped by a monument honouring Paraguay's Indigenous peoples. The city also has the Yacht y Golf Club Paraguayo, clubs, hotels and a wide range of shops, and is divided into 28 neighbourhoods.

    As it borders Asunción, Lambaré lets you live in a house or duplex, with more space, without moving away from the capital.
